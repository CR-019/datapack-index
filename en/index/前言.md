# Editor's Note

At the beginning of this year, MCBBS closed, which was a significant blow to vanilla mod development. The loss of a large number of useful tutorials and project resources was one problem. More seriously, the domestic data pack development ecosystem was already incomplete. Once the forum—a place that brought players from all walks of life together—disappeared, this field fell into complete disintegration. It is sometimes difficult to find comprehensive material to study, and finding communities for discussion has also become more difficult. This is clearly detrimental to data pack development.

In addition, Mojang made extensive changes to data packs and resource packs during the year, and many older tutorials are no longer applicable to newer versions. Explanations of the new versions are also scarce. As one can imagine, this will inevitably leave newcomers confused and make the current situation of data pack development even worse.

The community happened to propose the need for a data pack development guide, so I wanted to use this opportunity to organize the relevant resources, do my best to collect the tutorials I knew, and provide future data pack enthusiasts with some reference and help.

By coincidence, I learned about Xiao2's article *Data Pack Architecture*, which collected a considerable amount of material. Although it was written relatively early and cited much content from before MCBBS, it could still greatly reduce my workload. After discussion, we decided to use it as a template, repair missing links, and add features from newer versions. This site was born from that effort.

This documentation site is a repaired and expanded version of the original *Data Pack Architecture*. The specific changes are:
- Redirecting invalid MCBBS links;
- Fine-tuning the article structure and adding a practice section, whose content focuses more on example-based tutorials;
- Adding content related to newer versions.

However, this site is still far from complete. My knowledge is limited, so many sections contain little or no content and urgently need supplementation. We also failed to find all of the original MCBBS resources and had to delete links that could not be restored.
Furthermore, because the adaptation was done in a hurry, it inevitably contains imperfections and even mistakes. I hope everyone will point them out.
We welcome contributions of relevant material to help this site improve day by day.

In short, I hope to make a small contribution.
