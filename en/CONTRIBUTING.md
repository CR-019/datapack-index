# Vanilla Front-end Library — Submission Rules and Disclaimer

## 0. Preface and scope of application
These rules ("Rules") apply to all entries, indexes, descriptions and related materials submitted to the "Vanilla Library" (subpage of Vanilla Library, hereinafter referred to as "the Library" or "we") through the GitHub Issue form or in accordance with the library's procedures. The rules describe the relationship between inclusion, review, editing, display, deletion and rights and obligations; they apply to all submitters ("Submitters") and website users ("Users").

## 1. Definition
- Submitter: The individual or organization that submits the entry content or information to the library.
- Resources: indexed or explained entities or links such as pre-configurations, modules, libraries, data packs, tools, documentation, etc.
- Entry/index page: A record of the web pages displayed on the library based on the submitted content.
- Maintenance team: Library staff or volunteers responsible for reviewing, editing and publishing entries.

## 2. Submit Statement of Principles and Authorization
- Submitters should ensure that they have legal rights to the submitted content or have been expressly authorized by the rights holder.
- By submitting, the submitter grants the Library a **non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, sublicensable, modifiable (for display/formatting/indexing purposes only) license** to display, reproduce, cache and index on the Library and related services.
- Submission does not transfer copyright; the original rights holder may provide proof or request correction/deletion at any time (see Article 9).

## 3. Committer obligations (must be observed)
- **Authenticity**: The information provided must be true, accurate, and verifiable.
- **Rights and Sources**: For non-original content, the source and original author must be indicated, and the original license or link must be provided when possible.
- **Legality**: Do not submit illegal content (including but not limited to infringement, piracy, malware, invasion of privacy, child pornography, hate speech, incitement to violence, etc.).
- **Privacy Protection**: Do not include sensitive personal information of others (ID number, bank card number, private communication content, etc.) in public submissions. If you really need to submit privacy-related certification, please send a private message or email and mark it "for review only".
- **Security Statement**: Do not falsely claim that a security scan or scan results have been performed on a resource. If a scan report is provided, it must be accompanied by original evidence or a credible source.
- **Updates and Corrections**: If facts change or errors are discovered, submitters should promptly submit an update request.

## 4. Required fields and recommended templates
Submissions should contain at least the necessary fields from the form given, missing fields may result in delays or rejection of inclusion.

## 5. Review process and time limit (target time limit)
- Submission confirmation: We will confirm receipt or request additional information in the Issue within **3 working days** after receiving the materials.
- Preliminary review: With complete information, the target is to complete the preliminary compliance check (copyright, obviously illegal content, format) within **7 working days**.
- In-depth review and release: If additional verification is required, the goal is to complete it within **30 days** and decide whether to include, reject or mark as "requires verification".
- Urgent infringement or security complaints: After receiving the material and confirming that it is in complete form, we will take interim measures (removal or flagging) as soon as possible (usually within 48–72 hours) until the investigation is completed.

Note: The above time limit is a target commitment and may be extended depending on the complexity of manpower and evidence. We will update advancement in Issue in real time.

## 6. Editorial rights and display specifications
- We reserve the right to edit, merge, or delete submissions for uniform format, readability, and compliance, but will not change factual statements** (such as submitter statements, author information, license terms) unless authorized by the submitter or with clear evidence of error.
- We will keep the source link and submission record in the entry; important changes will be recorded in the issue summary.
- For entries that are controversial or pose security risks, we may add prominent warning tags (such as "Unverified", "May contain malicious code", etc.).

## 8. Intellectual Property Rights and Infringement Complaint ProcessAppeals require submission of written materials and sent in GitHub Issues. For efficient processing, please provide:

For infringement/removal requests (submitting party is the rights owner or its agent):
- Description and ownership certificate of the infringing work (certificate of original creation, copyright registration, source code warehouse ownership, etc.).
- The URL/page/submission instructions of the allegedly infringing entry.
- Description of specific infringement points (list the infringing content item by item).
- The requested processing action (delete/block/correct).
- Contact details.

Counter-notification (counter-claim) requirements (if the alleged infringing party believes that there is no infringement):
- Identity and contact email address, description of the content to be removed, a statement stating that the content does not constitute infringement in the reasonable belief, and a signature agreeing to accept information from the competent court and bear possible legal consequences.

Processing principles and time limits:
- For complete infringement complaints, we will confirm receipt within **3 working days** and take interim measures after verifying the necessity.
- Verification and final decision are based on facts and evidence; if legal advice is needed, we may ask the rights holder to make a decision through a court or authoritative arbitration.
- We are not responsible for indirect losses caused by incorrect removal, but will assist both parties in communication and review within a reasonable range.

## 9. Privacy and Personal Information Processing

- Do not expose unauthorized personal sensitive information on public pages.
