## Instructions for use

The Cartographer Pack (also known as the Big Pack) is a collection of multi-functional pre-packages built around CTM maps, allowing users to add customized spells, states, attributes, monster attacks, and other mechanisms to the map.

Read its [official wiki](https://github.com/pearuhdox/Cartographer-2.0/wiki) for more instructions.
