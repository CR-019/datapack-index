<InfoCard />

In Minecraft, player scores are recorded using their ID rather than UUID. This causes the player to lose all its points after changing its ID. This library can solve this problem. It can automatically migrate the player's score when the player changes his ID, and can also read the score of the offline player.

Read its official documentation ([Github](https://github.com/CMDred/ScoreFixer) | [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/datapack/score-fixer)) for details.
