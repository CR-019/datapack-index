VVE (Vanilla Vehicle Engine) is a vanilla Minecraft physics-engine data pack created by xiaodou8593 (`xiaodou123`), with an emphasis on performance and practical use.

Read its [official documentation](https://github.com/xiaodou8593/vve3.0) to learn how to use it.

VVE supports particle-based models, rigid bodies, and static surfaces such as slopes.\
It includes inertia-tensor calculations and precise performance controls, allowing hundreds of physical bodies to be simulated simultaneously.\
You can use VVE to build grenades, vehicles, dice, and other entities that rotate, move, bounce, and respond to gravity or buoyancy.
