> The Vanilla Award is an annual selection event established to select outstanding authors and articles in "Feature" and encourage everyone to submit more articles to "Feature". The award setting and selection are decided by the Vanilla Library working group (CR_019, Alumopper, Xiao2, Dahesor).

**Good morning/noon/evening everyone! Welcome to the 2025 Vanilla Awards! **
In the past 2025, we founded the vanilla development monthly magazine "Feature". In these 9 months, we have received a lot of submissions. On behalf of all members of the Vanilla Library working group, I would like to express my heartfelt thanks to everyone!
Many high-quality articles were produced among these submissions, and we hope to praise and encourage these articles and their authors by awarding Vanilla Awards.
Next, let’s see who wins the Vanilla Award!

## annual tutorial

> This award is used to select the most valuable works in the tutorial category.

**The nominated articles are:**

- Dahesor: [A brief description of data pack optimization principles and analysis methods](/en/feature/archive/202504/3/content.md)
> Reason for nomination: Data pack optimization has always been a hot issue in data pack development discussions. This article outlines the basic principles of data pack optimization and gives optimization methods and suggestions.
- Start a small business: [minecraft instructions cooking guide: cold entityselector](/en/feature/archive/202507/2/content.md)
> Reason for nomination: EntitySelector is a very basic and commonly used component in data pack development, but it is not easy to use it well. This article combines the mc source code to analyze the internal principles of entityselector in detail, and gives a clear and easy-to-understand conclusion on how to use entityselector as optimally as possible.
- Xuanyu 1725: [shader basic tutorial series](/en/feature/archive/202508/1/content.md)
> Reason for nomination: Shader has always been an "unknown territory" in vanilla development. Many developers know little about it, and some even regard it as a "wishing machine." This is undoubtedly due to the fact that its development difficulty is relatively separated from the data pack and the lack of tutorials. This series of serial tutorials analyzes the principles and usage of core shaders in a relatively easy-to-understand manner, filling a gap in the field.

## Conclusion
Thank you everyone for seeing this. At the end of the award ceremony, I would like to share some thoughts about "Feature".
Over the past year, Mojang has made many interesting updates, and many features have been put into practice in the submissions we received. This is the purpose of our creation of "Feature". If everyone comes to browse and learn something, this is enough for us.
The purpose of establishing the Vanilla Award this time is to encourage everyone to continue to submit articles to "Feature". If more vanilla creators pay attention to "Feature" and Vanilla Library, we can become more integrated into a community. One way is to submit articles. We also welcome everyone to express their opinions in the comment area of ​​each monthly magazine. This is also a part of communication.
In addition, we found that there is a phenomenon that developers have done very interesting practices, but they mistakenly believe that the submission requirements of "Feature" are very high and their submissions are "not qualified", so they dare not submit. I hope to correct this misunderstanding. On the homepage, we have always written "to facilitate communication among developers", so the submission threshold for "Feature" has always been very low. As long as you have interesting enough discoveries or practices, or have made a small work or toy, you can submit an article to "Feature". We'd love to help with your project.
**Finally, thank you again for your continued support of "Feature". This award ceremony ends here. We will see you next year! **
