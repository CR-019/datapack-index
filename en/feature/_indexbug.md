## Contribute to "Feature"
<ColorLine />

### The types of submissions we hope to get

Contributions must be related to MCvanilla development, and the type is not limited.
The following are possible submission types:
- Instance-based data pack analysis
- Research on some features of vanilla and practical applications
- Research on new snapshot features
- Data Experience in pack debugging and performance optimization
- Small work display and release

### Submission form

We accept various types of submissions. They can be original articles, links to articles posted on various blogs or forums, or videos, etc.
You can send the following information containing the submission content to 1703467028@qq.com :
- The main content of the submission must indicate the link to the article or video, or send the original text as an attachment;
- Manuscript title;
- Abstract (optional but recommended): describe the main content of the submission in brief text;
  - If the contributor does not provide an abstract, the editor will write an abstract himself when adopting.
__M DP53__Header image (optional but recommended): A picture used at the beginning of the document.
  - Beautiful pictures can attract readers' interest, and articles that provide a header image are more likely to be selected as cover articles.
- Video material or material link (optional but recommended): used as peripheral materials for promotional videos.
  - The editor does not have much time to experience the submitted content in detail. **About 15 seconds** of video material can greatly facilitate the time spent on video editing.
- Author signature, and (optional) social media account link, used to mark the author when displaying, and facilitate readers to contact the author;
