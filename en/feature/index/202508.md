# Featured

::: tip Editor's Note
This month we received several submissions about **terrain generation** and **shader**. As a niche area in data pack research, few people have covered these parts, and there are many gaps in the tutorials. However, the importance of these two areas is also obvious: developers can modify the world with a higher degree of freedom through them.
Therefore, we believe that these contributions are very valuable and have greatly enriched the research gaps in this field. We also encourage more developers to invest in exploring these less-explored parts.
:::
