Here is ***Vanilla*** news, the most ***Vanilla*** technical snapshot news in Minecraft. Our reporter *Vanilla Fox* reports the latest snapshot news for you~

This month Mojang released multiple pre and rcversions of 26.2, and the official version will be released in the near future. Currently, the data packversion has reached **107.1**, and the resource packversion has reached **88.0**.

Let me talk about the conclusion first. There are basically no updates this month. They are all minor repairs and repairs, and p2p connections have been rolled back. Overall, it is at a **medium level** level.

## Miscellaneous

* Added new world preset`flat_all_dimensions`。
* `execute on owner`The evoker who summons a vex is now considered the vex's owner.
*`charged_projectiles`The component now accepts a maximum stack of 1024 items.
* Added entity type tag`#not_affected_by_geysers`, representing an entity that is not affected by the upward impulse provided by a geyser eruption.
