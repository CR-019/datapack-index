Midnight Soul is an asymmetrical competitive map based on the Java Edition of Minecraft. As a soul or soul guardian, you will achieve the ultimate victory in evading or chasing.

## ◆ Gameplay
As a **soul**, you who desire to reshape your body need to collect scattered soul fragments and release the aura within them. When the surrounding aura is strong enough, the portal will appear - and by charging it before the aura dissipates, you can return to the present world through it, which we call "resurrection".

As a **soul guardian** who maintains balance in the world, what you need to do is simple: stop the soul. Whether it is knocking down the soul so that it cannot maintain its own existence, or blocking the way until the spiritual energy is completely exhausted, it can make them "die" and eventually return to the earth as residual memories.

The brave one wins when they meet on a narrow road. In the final game, the number of resurrected souls and dead souls will be compared, and the one with better results will win!

In addition, there are many special mechanisms in the game such as **Aura, Breath Detection, Soul Lamp, Soul Treasure Box**, etc., waiting for you to experience it!

## ◆ Map features
- **A large number of abilities**: There are 31 unique abilities in the current version, and they are being continuously updated...
- **Various Maps**: There are already 6 maps with different themes in the game, and there are even 9 custom map slots! [[See here]](https://www.bilibili.com/opus/1210032492206096404)
- **Random events**: 10 global effects triggered with probability will bring a very different gaming experience
- **Audio-visual design**: Whether it is the game itself or peripheral configurations, there are sound effects and visual effects to match it.
- **player incentives**: We have a complete set of algorithms and several decorations dedicated to players who want to pursue high scores or obtain rewards.
- **Fully balanced**: After long-term testing and balance adjustments, various mechanisms in the game are quite balanced and will bring a good gaming experience to players.
- **Highly controllable**: By calling the debugging panel in the game, you can control the entire game with a high degree of freedom!

## ◆ Other matters
- The **Debug Panel** mentioned above needs to be run within the game.`/function debug:0`to make a call
- Midnight Soul uses advancement to store decoration unlock status, so you can`/advancement grant @a from main:extra/root`to unlock all decorations for all players
- In the Midsoul data pack`base`This section contains various "wheels" used in this map. You can use them by yourself if necessary.
