## Lava Chicken Update

In 1.21.6-rc1, Mojang added the *Lava Chicken* record from Hyper Potions to Minecraft, dropped by the player or a player-tamed wolf that kills baby zombies that are riding chickens. This song comes from the soundtrack in the Minecraft movie, and there was also the more famous [Camellia Remix version] (https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1x3LUzPE8j).
NetEase Cloud of this song: &lt;https://music.163.com/song?id=2717690420&gt;

::: 1.21.6
Author information:

Hyper Potions is an electronic dance music band from California, USA. Ian Tsuchiura is the only member of the band. The band created the album Minecraft: Lava Chicken (Original Game Soundtrack) for a music record of Minecraft. He also created the remix albums Minecraft: Pixel Genesis and Minecraft: Triple Bundle to promote Minecraft. \
